1. holders_count — Token holder count
   Number of distinct wallets currently holding a token.
   path: tokens/holders
   Returns the count of unique addresses with a non-zero balance of the given token at the latest indexed block. Counts addresses, not people: one person may hold from several wallets, and contract, burn and exchange addresses are included unless you exclude them.
   input: address (string, required) — Token contract address or Solana mint.
          chain (string, one of solana|ethereum|base|bsc, default "solana") — Which chain the token lives on.
          excludeContracts (boolean, default false) — Leave out contract and burn addresses.
   returns: An integer count and the block height it was measured at.
   call: GET https://data.example.com/v1/tokens/{address}/holders/count?chain={chain}&exclude_contracts={excludeContracts}  (auth: x-api-key header, injected by your own backend)
   cost: standard
   example: {"address":"So11111111111111111111111111111111111111112"}  (wSOL on Solana)
   see also: holders_percentage (alternative) — share of supply per holder, not a count
   see also: holders_top (pairs_with) — who the largest holders actually are
   see also: token_resolve_symbol (prerequisite) — if you only have a ticker, not an address

2. holders_top — Largest holders
   The biggest wallets holding a token, with balance and share of supply.
   path: tokens/holders
   input: address (string, required) — Token contract address or Solana mint.
          chain (string, one of solana|ethereum|base|bsc, default "solana")
          limit (integer, default 20) — How many wallets to return.
   returns: A ranked list of addresses with balance, share of supply and first-seen date.
   call: GET https://data.example.com/v1/tokens/{address}/holders/top?chain={chain}&limit={limit}  (auth: x-api-key header, injected by your own backend)
   cost: expensive — Scans the full holder set on large tokens.
   see also: wallet_pnl (pairs_with) — what those wallets actually made on the token
   see also: holders_count (pairs_with) — who the largest holders actually are
   see also: holders_percentage (pairs_with) — the actual addresses behind the percentages

3. holders_percentage — Holder distribution
   What share of supply each holder controls, as top-N or bucketed percentages.
   path: tokens/holders
   Returns the concentration of ownership: the percentage of circulating supply held by the top 1, 10, 50 and 100 wallets, plus an optional per-wallet breakdown. This is the number people usually mean when they ask whether a token is 'concentrated' or 'distributed'.
   input: address (string, required) — Token contract address or Solana mint.
          chain (string, one of solana|ethereum|base|bsc, default "solana")
          buckets (array, default [1,10,50,100]) — Top-N cutoffs to report.
   returns: A percentage of circulating supply per bucket, and the supply figure used.
   call: GET https://data.example.com/v1/tokens/{address}/holders/distribution?chain={chain}  (auth: x-api-key header, injected by your own backend)
   cost: standard
   see also: holders_top (pairs_with) — the actual addresses behind the percentages
   see also: holders_count (alternative) — share of supply per holder, not a count
